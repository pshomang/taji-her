The concepts

Every card gives you the exact opening line, the six-beat retention map with timecodes, what to point the camera at, the overlays, the interrupts, the payoff, and the moment most likely to lose people.

Two rules that protect the account. Every rate, applicant count and payout in these scripts has to come from a live screen recording you took yourself on the day, not from memory and not from another creator's video — the screen recording is also what makes the video work, so this costs you nothing and buys you everything.

Second: never fake the dashboard, the rejection email or the client reply. This niche runs on proof, and one fabricated screenshot is the end of the channel. If a number is embarrassing, use it — the honest version consistently outperforms the polished one here.
